## Patterns worth using
- **Night activity separates attackers almost perfectly.** All three attack IPs act between 18:00 and 05:00 UTC (40 events). Benign night events number only 5 (late logouts).
- **The low-and-slow attacker evades R1.** 192.0.2.77 has the most failures (15), but at 1 every 3 minutes it never trips the 5-per-5-minute rule.
- **R2 catches the spray first.** 198.51.100.23 hits 8 different usernames, and R2 fires 20 s before R1.
